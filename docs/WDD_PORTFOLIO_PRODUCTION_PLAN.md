## Asset standard

- Editable coded webpage source for every concept
- Master PNG: 1440 px wide and approximately 8200–9000 px tall
- Optimized full-page WebP at matching proportions
- Dedicated 1200 × 1500 WebP thumbnail
- Complete page from navigation through footer
- No browser chrome
- Original concept identity and logo
- No fabricated clients, testimonials, statistics, results, awards, or launches
- Every gallery item must visibly remain a DESIGN CONCEPT

## Release gates

- Individual founder approval at full-page scale
- Full-page lightbox supports vertical scrolling
- Previous and next controls remain available
- Keyboard, focus, Escape, and screen-reader status behavior verified
- Responsive verification at 1440, 1024, 768, 390, and 320 px
- No clipping, overflow, unreadable copy, or duplicated hero composition
- Lint, typecheck, tests, asset verification, and production build pass
